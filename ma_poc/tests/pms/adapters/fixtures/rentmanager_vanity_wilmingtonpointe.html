Welcome Home to Wilmington Pointe!

We offer a convenient location and spacious one and two bedroom apartment homes in several different styles.

We are conveniently located in South Dayton just on the outskirts of both Kettering and Oakwood near the quiet residential neighborhoods of Patterson Park and Shroyer Park. Our community features a sparkling swimming pool with sundeck, onsite laundry facilities, city bus at your door and ample parking.

You'll enjoy quick access to I-75, SR-35, downtown Dayton, Miami Valley Hospital, The University of Dayton, Sinclair Community College and nearby suburbs Kettering and Oakwood. Local shopping and dining is also within walking distance.
