Let Our Historic Baltimore Apartments Elevate Your Life

You will love calling any of our apartments in Mount Vernon home. Ranging between 550 and 1,100 square feet, each layout has been thoughtfully designed to provide you with an outstanding living experience. You’ll feel the comforts through the freshly renovated designs with breathtaking city views, fully equipped kitchens with stainless-steel appliances, and full-size washers/dryers, among other amenities ready to elevate your routine.
 

Studio

With 550 square feet, this studio is perfect for anyone seeking an efficient space in the heart of the city.
 

One-Bedrooms

The one-bedroom options range from 550 to 850 square feet.
 

Two-Bedrooms

Medium-sized households appreciate our two-bedroom layouts, which boast an additional bathroom and up to 1,050 square feet.
 

Three-Bedrooms

If you’re looking for even more room to roam, the three-bedroom residence is for you, offering 1,100 square feet of pure comfort.

We can’t wait to show you around these apartments in Baltimore, Maryland, so call us today!
